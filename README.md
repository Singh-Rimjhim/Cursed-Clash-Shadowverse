# Cursed Clash:Shadowverse


**A Multi-Mode Cursed Spirit Fighting & Civilian Rescue Browser Game**

Cursed Clash: Shadowverse is a browser-based action game inspired by supernatural anime battles, cursed energy, and domain-style abilities. Players take on the role of an original sorcerer, fight dangerous cursed spirits, protect civilians, and survive increasingly challenging encounters.

The game features a pseudo-3D arena experience built entirely with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the HTML Canvas API — without requiring external image assets.

## Game Modes

### 1. Veilbreaker Rescue
- Fight cursed spirits threatening the city.
- Move through the arena and defeat approaching enemies.
- Rescue civilians and complete the evacuation mission.
- Secure the district by saving all civilians.

### 2. Endless Curse Night
- Survive waves of increasingly challenging cursed spirits.
- Earn experience points by defeating enemies.
- Unlock upgrades to improve combat performance.
- Choose between increased attack damage, health recovery, and faster cursed energy generation.

### 3. Domain Duel
- Face a powerful special-grade boss.
- Learn enemy attack patterns and dodge incoming attacks.
- Build cursed energy through combat.
- Unleash your ultimate domain technique to deal massive damage.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Mode Gameplay:** Three different mission types in one browser game.
- **Real-Time Combat:** Attack, jump, dash, and unleash ranged energy bursts.
- **Cursed Energy System:** Build energy during combat and use powerful abilities.
- **Domain Expansion:** Activate a special ultimate attack when energy reaches 100%.
- **Civilian Rescue Mechanics:** Complete objectives while fighting hostile spirits.
- **Wave-Based Survival:** Face progressively more difficult enemy encounters.
- **Upgradeable Abilities:** Improve damage, health, and energy generation.
- **Pseudo-3D Arena:** Perspective floor, depth-inspired character placement, and atmospheric effects.
- **Custom Visual Effects:** Canvas-generated particles, glowing attacks, and domain effects.
- **Responsive Interface:** Anime-inspired dark UI with neon purple, pink, and cyan accents.
- **No Image Assets Required:** Characters, arenas, and visual effects are drawn programmatically.

## Combat System

**Violet Thread — Basic Attack:** Strike nearby cursed spirits to deal damage and generate cursed energy.

**Violet Burst — Ranged Attack:** Spend 20% cursed energy to launch a projectile at enemies.

**Threadstep — Dash:** Move quickly across the arena and gain a brief window of invulnerability.

**Violet Thread Sanctum — Domain Expansion:** Consume 100% cursed energy to unleash a powerful area attack against active enemies.
